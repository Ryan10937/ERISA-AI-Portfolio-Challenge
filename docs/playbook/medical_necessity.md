# Medical Necessity (CO-50 style)

**CARC CO-50 definition (summary):** service not deemed 'medical necessity' by the payer.

## Workup approach
- Extract what service was billed (CPT) and what condition was treated (ICD).
- If ICD codes are missing, list as an open question and request clinical evidence.
- Produce a doc request plan:
  - H&P, progress notes
  - imaging reports (if relevant)
  - conservative therapy history
  - surgeon/provider rationale

## Narrative rules
- Avoid legal citations for this challenge; focus on clinical rationale and missing evidence.
